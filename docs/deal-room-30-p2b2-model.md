# P2b.2 — dated legal research and bounded calculation contract

Research date: 2026-10-16. Base: PR #31. No production or paid service.

## Primary sources and temporal limits

- PIT: [Dz.U. 2026 poz. 592, updated Sejm text](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2026/592/text/U/D20260592Lj.pdf), edition 2026-09-16, including listed amendments 779/846/1079/1098/1123. Art. 10(1)(8), 19, 22(6c–6f), 30e govern eligible private disposals; 9a/30c business linear PIT; 30a(1)(4) natural-person dividends. Eligibility, taxable basis and reliefs are distinct from economic profit. No automatic private classification/five-year/own-housing relief.
- CIT: [Dz.U. 2026 poz. 554, updated Sejm text](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2026/554/text/U/D20260554Lj.pdf), edition 2026-10-08. Art. 19(1)(1) standard 19%; reduced 9% has separate current/prior turnover, formation and income-source conditions. No automatic 9%, Estonian CIT or investor tax split.
- VAT: [Dz.U. 2026 poz. 1263](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2026/1263/text/T/D20261263L.pdf), published 2026-09-28. The previous consolidated announcement 775/2025 expired on that date; this does NOT mean the VAT Act expired. Art. 15, 41, 43, 86, 91, 146ef distinguish taxable person, supply/rates, exemption, deduction and adjustments. Amendment [896/2025](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2025/896/ogl) enters 2026-01-01. An old search snippet is not a valid threshold. No automatic exemption, input deduction or refund.
- PCC: [Dz.U. 2026 poz. 191](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2026/191/text/T/D20260191L.pdf), legal state 2026-02-16. Art. 6 uses market value; art. 7 standard property rate 2%; art. 7a conditional sixth/subsequent unit 6%. Art. 2(4) VAT relationship and art. 9 exceptions must be established. Neither exempt VAT nor buying a first flat automatically yields zero PCC in this model.
- Rounding: [Ordynacja podatkowa, updated 622/2026](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2026/622/text/U/D20260622Lj.pdf), art. 63. General full-zloty base/tax rounding must not be confused with invoice VAT cents or interest rounding exceptions.

These edition/publication dates are NOT dates when all individual rules entered into force. The amendment/transition audit is incomplete. No complete effective interval for executable transaction qualification has been certified. Consequently all actual liabilities, net project profit, payout, post-tax cash-flow, ROI, Max Buy and stress MUST remain UNKNOWN. No professional review is claimed. This stage may expose explicitly conditional arithmetic of a selected rule and supplied tax basis; it must never promote that arithmetic to a verified liability or subtract it from P2a automatically.

## Calculation boundary and independent vectors, before implementation

Use integer cents; general annual income/PCC base rounded to PLN, then percentage tax rounded to PLN (half up). VAT gross split is invoice arithmetic to cents, not a tax-return settlement. Inputs max PLN 100 million, finite nonnegative cents, no coercion, no negative refunds. Unsupported rule/date/basis -> UNKNOWN. Zero must be explicit.

Independent decimal vectors (not generated by the TypeScript implementation):
- base PLN 100000 -> 19% PIT or standard CIT PLN 19000.
- company example only: accounting profit = taxable base = 100000; CIT 19000 leaves 81000; an independently lawful full dividend of 81000 to a Polish resident natural person incurs 15390, leaves 65610. This is NOT authority to distribute cash or capital and is not automatically applied to the selected deal.
- PCC market value 300000 -> standard 2% = 6000; qualifying special 6% = 18000 (not 2% + 6%). A contract price of 260000 does not replace market value.
- annual base 100.49 -> rounded 100 -> 19; 100.50 -> 101 -> 19; 102.50 -> 103 -> 20. Dividend gross amounts must retain cents when reporting net cash, even when tax base is rounded.
- invoice gross 123000 at explicitly applicable 23% -> net 100000 / VAT 23000; gross 108000 at 8% -> 100000 / 8000. The entire renovation cannot be silently divided by a chosen rate.

## User flow and rollback

With isolated QA, CFO, TAX_REVIEW and TAX_ARITHMETIC flags enabled, complete the existing tax questions, select a supported rule and enter its independently established basis as a decimal string (`100000.00`). A saved assumption remains NEEDS_EVIDENCE. The panel and CEO risk explain the conditional amount, legal provision, source edition and unresolved applicability; the model does not label it tax due. Withdrawing the basis removes arithmetic, receipt replay returns the original immutable round, and reload fetches the current round. Switching the new flag off and refreshing removes the projection; answers/history survive. No schema migration, new authorization grant or service is introduced.
