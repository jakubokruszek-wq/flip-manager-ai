IDs are never decoded into numbers, guessed from expected URL, matched by suffix or joined globally across payloads. Missing/different opaque IDs, foreign links, conflicting numeric IDs, comments/feedback/shared/quoted ancestors and invalid JSON remain rejected. At most 20 sources and 8 opaque constraints/source; overflow fails closed. No payload AST, source export or additional fetch.

Before pixels are captured, actual loaded IMG natural dimensions, rendered dimensions, exact currentSrc/src, unique article, foreign-header exclusion, focus/visibility/occlusion and two-phase viewport proof are still mandatory.
