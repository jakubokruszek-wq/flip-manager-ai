# Gecko POC 0.5.43 — broader local OCR coverage

Physical 0.5.42 result: two posts, six images, two OCR suggestions, no errors; eleven manual-review posts remained. This demonstrates local OCR execution, not complete data coverage.

The viewport card selector previously rejected every data-testid containing `comment`, including `commentable_feed_story`. Align the two viewport selectors with the existing message/photo exclusions: that exact post-container name is permitted, while actual comment containers remain excluded. Exact post/group ownership, top-level card scope, network ownership where required, visibility, geometry, source stability, native consent and two-phase crop checks remain mandatory.

Feed capture now prefers posts without a committed crop, then unresolved rows, and reserves one committed image per post in the feed pass, rather than spending multiple capture frames on the same post. Failed or dropped captures may retry; only committed images count. This favors post coverage over a multi-photo gallery. The existing frame/image/time limits are unchanged. Native OCR cache keeps its 20-image/24-MiB bounds and may recycle an extra view of a post to admit the first crop of another post; it never removes a post's only OCR crop for this purpose. OCR processes manual-review rows first and visits every available post once before extra images. Text and OCR remain separate; no automatic qualification or upload.

Offers shows the count of manual-review posts with an available OCR crop. A missing crop is not evidence of missing property data. Styled text-only backgrounds, unbound posts, offscreen/unloaded media or unsupported DOM can still be unavailable. No claim that all eleven physical posts are recovered.

Validation: Node regression tests, CI isolation checks, app/instrumented compilation, JVM tests and signed APK. Long emulator execution is intentionally skipped per the user's preference. The new bitmap-cache instrumentation test is compiled, not executed in the fast lane. No real Facebook/group test by the agent.
