## Corrections

0.5.12 remembered the feed after actual DOM movement but still accepted hits
only in visible cards or semantic main/feed markup. Blank padding or a loading
sentinel at its end could therefore disable input indefinitely. Within the SAME
trial, an ordinary wheel can now target the visible rectangle of that previously
moved feed even when all its articles are offscreen. The target must still be
connected, in the same document, contain current top-level articles, and contain
the actual rendered hit within its rectangle. A bounded ancestor walk rejects
nested scroll widgets without article content, menus/listboxes/complementary
regions. Existing dialog/comment/iframe/form/sidebar guards remain. Unknown or
unmoved containers gain no such permission. This does not confirm any post ID.

Thumbnail slots were reserved before checking visibility/occlusion. Three loaded
but offscreen pictures could fill the three slots and exclude a later visible
image forever without even proposing a screenshot. Reserve a slot only after
all existing loaded/visible/area/occlusion and exact-association checks pass.
No weakening of the 60% visibility, pixel/frame/source/ID/context confirmation,
trusted media association, 3-per-post or 20-post bounds. Failed or ambiguous
association remains a refusal. No new media downloads or gallery traversal.
