# Gecko POC 0.5.37 — opening unresolved posts before OCR

0.5.36 could only OCR previously retained feed crops. A physical Samsung report showed three ordinary photos from one post while many unresolved posts had no crops. This release adds a fresh consented, foreground exact-link pass from the manual-review queue. It does not change normal feed scrolling.

In Offers, **Otwórz brakujące dane + OCR (N)** appears for manual-review rows even without any feed thumbnail. The fresh grant explicitly covers opening their exact permalinks, reading DOM/embedded records, and locally recognising a rendered photo. This does not reuse old review or scan consent. The plain text-enrichment button remains OCR-free.

The current RAM queue is capped at 20 exact posts of the approved group. Empty-text posts, then least-complete rows, come first. The temporary visible GeckoSession shares the existing runtime and restores the original group afterwards. Existing exact navigation/location/port-generation, focus, visibility and login/verification checks remain active. Only a bound main post can trigger media examination; URL identity alone is insufficient.

The separate content reader considers loaded, sufficiently large IMG elements in the exact post article. A sibling image may belong to a dialog/main region only when the region contains exactly one article and all post links identify that same group and post. Comments, forms, quoted/shared cards and other articles are excluded. Up to three candidate images are considered locally; at most one image per post is captured so an early gallery cannot consume the whole 20-image budget. Alt text only ranks candidates and never supplies fields or identity.

An image is centred without clicking a link. Its visible rectangle is checked against the top hit at three points; capture uses the existing official GeckoView pixel bridge. Geometry, context and owner are rechecked before committing the crop. The full captured frame is recycled; only that image crop enters the bundled on-device recognizer, with an 8-second wait bound. Temporary crops are cleared after each post. No extra image requests, gallery traversal, media downloads, image URLs or recognised text are persisted/exported. A missed or unreadable image remains unresolved.

OCR suggestions are projected in isolated extension background, exact-ID checked and shown separately as **OCR — DO SPRAWDZENIA**. They never overwrite the text-enrichment result, auto-qualify an offer, or enter a collector. The entire opened-post pass has the existing 3-minute deadline, at most 20 images. Context loss/pause/cancellation stops and discards preview/suggestions. Only aggregate fixed counters remain in the separate OCR and enrichment histories. A model task owns and eventually recycles its copied bitmap even after cancellation.

## Verification and Samsung

New Node regressions cover two-phase capture, absent fresh OCR consent, login/hidden/foreign contexts, exact URL without card proof, comment permalinks/media, unloaded images, occlusion, changed context after capture/drop, replay and sibling-region binding. JVM regression checks missing-data queue priority without identity changes. Native API36 regression opens exact loopback posts, captures a sibling generated image, executes the real bundled OCR and restores the original group. Existing offline-model, consent, pause, isolation and full suites remain required.

Install the signed update without uninstalling or clearing data. Run a fresh normal local preview, open Offers, choose **Otwórz brakujące dane + OCR**, and give the new grant. Keep the app/screen visible while exact posts open. Compare text fields and separate OCR suggestions to each original post. Read both histories in Tools/report. Report selected posts/images/read text/suggestions plus any stop reason; no need to share private post content.

Limits: only current exact IDs from the last preview are queued; only one loaded, visible IMG crop per bound post, no SVG/CSS OCR, no gallery download/OCR completeness guarantee. Photos without writing can produce no fields. 3-minute deadline can stop before 20 posts on slow pages. Actual Samsung/Facebook coverage requires operator comparison. No collector, production upload/LIVE, backend changes, merge or deployment.
